## The six stories

1. **Anthropic's record IPO** — the AI lab's shares debut on Nasdaq in
   the largest IPO ever: $81bn raised, shares open 34% up at $111.50,
   valuing the company near $2tn. (Reuters, WSJ, Barron's, The Times)
2. **Myanmar guilty of genocide** — the International Court of Justice
   rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority.
   (BBC, Le Monde)
3. **COP31 drops fossil fuels** — the climate summit in Antalya, Türkiye
   ends with a draft deal that removes the fossil-fuel phase-out and the
   $120bn adaptation-finance target. (Reuters, Dawn, The Hindu)
4. **Senate rejects Iran curb** — the US Senate votes 48–51 against a
   war-powers resolution that would have curbed strikes on Iran.
   (Reuters, Al Jazeera)
5. **Missiles hit Kyiv** — Russia launches ballistic missiles at the
   Ukrainian capital overnight; fires burn in three districts.
   (Kyiv Post, Kyiv Independent, Reuters)
6. **65,000 tents wait for Gaza** — approved winter shelter sits outside
   Gaza awaiting entry as cold weather nears. (Scoop)

## Files

- `draw.py` — Pillow program that draws `entry.png` (build time only).
- `entry.png` — the image, 1024×1024 PNG.
- `main.py` — stdlib-only entry program: copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, byte-identical on every run.
